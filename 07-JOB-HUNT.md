# Job hunting alongside GATE

Reserve 08:00–10:00 on weekdays for this. Sending before 10–12 may help you stay consistent, but there is no need to believe every Indian company reads applications at one exact hour. Quality and follow-up matter more.

## Daily routine

- 20 minutes: find 3–5 relevant roles that fit your current CS skills.
- 50 minutes: tailor and submit 2 strong applications. Keep the resume truthful and aligned to each role.
- 30 minutes: identify 2 employees or alumni for referrals or informational outreach.
- 20 minutes: send follow-ups and update the tracker.

Spend one evening per week on coding interview practice or a small project relevant to your target roles; count algorithm practice shared with GATE where it genuinely overlaps.

## Referral message template

Hello [name], I am a CS graduate interested in the [role] opening at [company]. I have worked on [specific relevant project/skill], and the role's [specific requirement] matches my experience. If you are comfortable referring me, I can send my resume and the job link. Either way, I would appreciate any advice on the team. Thank you, [your name].

Personalise the bracketed parts; do not mass-send identical text. A polite follow-up after about a week is enough.

## Tracker columns

Use job-search.csv: company, role, URL, date applied, person contacted, response, next follow-up, notes. On weekends, review which role types get responses and adjust your resume or targeting.
