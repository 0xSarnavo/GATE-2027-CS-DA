## 6. Gaps — explicitly not filled (null / omitted, never guessed)

- **Rank-wise marks for AIR 100 / 500 / 1000 / 2000:** not published by any organising institute. Official sources publish only AIR-1 marks/scores plus paper-wise mark/score distributions in the statistical reports. No entries fabricated; `toppers` contains AIR-1 only.
- **2026-cycle programme (COAP 2026) cutoffs:** not found published at access time; no entries added.
- **2024 and 2026 programme cutoffs for IITH / IITG / IITR / IITKGP:** not found in official lists consulted; omitted rather than guessed (only the 2025 official lists above were located, plus the year-unstated IITH COAP table recorded with `year: null`).
- **IITH COAP round-wise PDF year:** the PDF itself carries no admission-year label, so `year` is `null` with an explanatory note.
- **IITR figures are normalised marks (out of 100), not GATE scores (out of 1000)** — flagged in each note.
- **IISc 2025 operated figures are Round-1 UR-Low admitted scores**, distinct from shortlist/eligibility cut-offs sometimes quoted by coaching sites.
