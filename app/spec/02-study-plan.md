## Macro goals per week

Each week in `plan.json` carries `macro_goals` with `{text, metric, target}`. Metrics used: `topics_completed`, `pyqs_solved`, `mocks_taken`, `flashcards_reviewed`, `error_log_entries`, `applications_sent` (habit/exam weeks add `study_days`, `diagnostic_papers`, `recall_days`, etc.). Conventions:

- A topic is complete when: study block done, topic PYQs attempted (15-20 shared/DA, 8-10 CS), half-page note written, mistakes logged.
- PYQ count is estimated at 8 min per question of PYQ block time; flashcards at 2 cards per minute. Counts are targets, not quotas.
- Weekly default: 10 topics, about 105 PYQs, 1 timed test, about 180 flashcards, 20 error-log entries, 10 applications.

## Daily task template (full plan, Mon-Fri)

| Time | Task | Type | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07:15-07:45 | GA practice (Mon/Wed/Fri, cycling verbal, quant, analytical, spatial) or flashcards (Tue/Thu) | study / flashcards | 2 |
| 08:00-10:00 | Job hunt: 3-5 roles, 2 tailored applications, 2 outreaches, follow-ups, tracker (`07-JOB-HUNT.md`) | job | 1 |
| 10:30-13:00 | Deep block: new shared/DA topic (max 2 concept videos + 1 worked example, half-page note) | study | 1 |
| 14:00-16:00 | PYQs on that topic, log every mistake | pyq | 2 |
| Fri 16:00-16:45 | Coding-interview practice or small project | job | 3 |
| 17:00-19:00 | Gym (travel and meal absorbed) | gym | 2 |
| 19:30-20:15 | Second subject: new CS topic | study | 1 |
| 20:15-20:45 | CS PYQs on that topic | pyq | 2 |
| 21:00-21:15 | Error log + spaced revision (1-day, 1-week, 3-week buckets computed per date) | revision | 1 |
| 21:15-21:30 | Check-in: set tomorrow's three tasks | checkin | 1 |

Study total is 6.5 h (390 min) plus job, gym and the check-in. In review weeks (11-14) the deep block becomes "review + timed set" per syllabus section, and full-paper days replace the deep and PYQ blocks (3 h paper 10:30-13:30, 2 h analysis 14:00-16:00).

Priority meaning: 1 = must-do (new-topic study, mocks and their analysis, job, spaced mistake revision); 2 = important (PYQ sets, GA, flashcards, gym, Sunday repair); 3 = optional (Sunday buffer, interview practice, outing).

## Weekend template

- **Saturday:** 07:15 flashcard sweep (30); 09:30 timed test (60-180 min per calendar); catch-up PYQs if time remains; 14:00 analysis (60-120 min, classify each error as concept, calculation, reading, time or guess); gym; optional outing 19:30. Study about 5.5 h.
- **Sunday:** 10:00 mistake review of last 1-3 weeks (60); 11:15 weak-topic repair (120); 14:30 **Sunday buffer** (60, priority 3, absorbs overflow); 16:30 weekly review (metrics vs targets, plan next week); 20:00 job tracker review. No gym. Study about 4 h. In paper weeks (13-14) Sunday holds a second full paper plus analysis.

## Revision cadence

1. Same day: half-page note + mistakes logged at 21:00.
2. Spaced buckets: each night the 21:00 task lists topics studied 1 day, 7 days and 21 days ago; redo only the logged mistakes.
3. Weekly: Sunday mistake review plus Saturday deck sweep.
4. Timed tests: sectional per Saturday (Nov-Dec), full paper from Sat 9 Jan, two papers per week from week 13 (Tue DA, Thu CS, Sat, Sun).
5. Final week: formula sheets and the top-20 repeated mistakes only.

## Adaptive rules

Definitions: a day is **missed** when less than 50% of its planned study minutes are done by 23:59. "Slack" is the extra time available in a day, never more than +60 min above that day's planned study minutes. Tasks of type `job` and `gym` are fixed and never moved.

### 1. A day is missed
1. Collect the unfinished study tasks of the day (types study, pyq, revision, flashcards, mock).
2. Sort by priority (1 first), then by original time, then by topic order in the week.
3. Spread them over the remaining days of the **same week** (Mon-Sat first, then Sunday): fill the day with the most room first, adding at most **+60 min per day** over the day's plan. Keep each task whole where possible; split only tasks of 90 min or more into two halves.
4. Never move a task onto a day earlier than its topic prerequisite (e.g. PYQs go after their study block).
5. If the week overflows: push priority-3 items first, then priority-2, into the Sunday buffer (60 min); anything still unplaced moves to **next week's Monday-Tuesday slack** (still +60 min cap) and is **flagged** (`overflow` badge on the week; app shows "N tasks pushed"). Priority-1 tasks are never dropped silently; if they cannot be placed, switch to catch-up mode.
6. Missed mocks and papers are rescheduled to the nearest free half-day (Sunday afternoon first); the next week's analysis block is not shortened.
7. Spaced-revision buckets for the missed day are recomputed from the new study date.
8. Missed days in the Puja window or the habit phase are never redistributed; they only reset the streak message.

### 2. Available hours change
- Let planned = the day's planned study minutes (weekday 390), new = user-set hours x 60.
- **Increase**: add minutes in this order: (a) extend PYQ blocks (up to +30 min each), (b) pull the next day's priority-2 task forward, (c) add a timed mixed set, (d) bring next week's first topic forward. Cap total at 8.5 h/day and keep sleep >= 6 h; warn above 8 h.
- **Decrease**: drop priority-3 first, then trim PYQ blocks to a minimum of 10 Qs per topic, then move priority-2 items (GA, flashcards) to the Sunday buffer; new-topic study blocks are shortened last and never below 90 min. If the weekly priority-1 load no longer fits, show how many topic-days the plan slips and suggest catch-up mode.
- A change may be scoped: today only, this week, or from a date onward. Changing the gym or job window re-times blocks but keeps the order.

### 3. Catch-up mode
Enter when: two or more consecutive missed days, more than 8 priority-1 tasks overdue, or the weekly topic completion is below 60% for two weeks.
- Freeze new mock scheduling except the Saturday paper.
- Suspend optional (priority 3) items; replace Tuesday/Thursday flashcards with spaced revision only if there is time.
- Reorder remaining topics by value: shared topics first (probability, linear algebra, DBMS, DS and algorithms basics), then DA-only (ML, AI) and the CS subjects with the most weight, using topic PYQ counts.
- Compress each topic to the minimum loop: one video, a half-page note, 10 PYQs.
- Use Sunday as a full catch-up day (buffer expands to 4 h) and add up to +60 min on weekdays.
- Exit when overdue priority-1 count is below 3 and the current topic matches the calendar; show a "back on plan" message.
- Never compress the final taper: cut earlier topics instead.

## Seed data schema notes

- `phases[]`: `{id, name, start, end, description}`.
- `weeks[]`: `{week_no, doc_week, label, start, end, phase_id, macro_goals[{text,metric,target}], focus{shared_da[], cs[]}}`. `focus.cs` lists the second-subject slot, which includes shared items such as stacks and GA in early weeks; in review weeks IDs may be section roots such as `prob`.
- `days[]`: `{date, tasks[{title, subject, topic_id|null, type, minutes, priority, time_block|null}]}`, covering every date from 2026-10-07 to 2027-02-21. Exam-day tasks have `time_block: null` because session timings are not announced.
- `topics.json`: 16 section roots plus leaf topics; `paper` is CS, DA, shared or GA per the overlap map in `03-SYLLABUS-AND-OVERLAP.md`; `videos[]` come from `04-VIDEOS.md` and are empty where that file names no source (calculus, discrete maths, GA, AI search and inference, warehouse, Python, C).
- Topic IDs use a subject prefix (`prob.bayes`, `la.eigen`, `os.sync`) rather than a paper prefix, so a topic keeps its ID regardless of which paper uses it.
