# Study plan: 26 October 2026 to GATE 2027

This is an aggressive two-paper plan. Treat 6 February as the first possible exam. Job search remains a daily task; gym and a short weekend outing stay in the calendar.

## Repeatable weekday

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 07:00–08:00 | Wake, food, 30 minutes of recall or General Aptitude |
| 08:00–10:00 | Applications, tailored resume, referrals, recruiter messages |
| 10:30–13:00 | Deep study block: new topic and examples |
| 13:00–14:00 | Lunch and break |
| 14:00–16:00 | PYQs and problem solving on that topic |
| 16:00–19:00 | Gym, travel, shower, meal (adjust to your actual gym window) |
| 19:30–21:30 | Second subject, revision or timed set |
| 21:30–22:00 | Error log and tomorrow's three tasks |

This allows about 6–6.5 focused study hours plus job hunting. Shift the clock as needed; preserve the order. Six hours of sleep is your stated minimum, but aim for 7 when possible because recall and accuracy matter.

## Weekend

Saturday: one 3-hour paper or sectional test, 2 hours analysis, and a 1–2-hour outing if wanted. Sunday: 3–4 hours of weak-topic repair, weekly planning, and the rest flexible. If an interview or outing moves a block, reschedule the block within the week; do not double the next day.

## Paper and revision method

- During topic study: solve topic-wise CS PYQs and DA chapter questions immediately.
- Every Sunday: 45–60 minutes on mistakes from the previous weeks.
- From early January: one full paper of each subject weekly, then increase if review time permits.
- Full paper: 3 hours timed, then at least 2 hours analysis. Classify mistakes as concept, calculation, reading, time, or guess.
- Keep separate CS and DA score sheets. Track raw score, attempted marks, accuracy, and unfinished questions. Avoid comparing raw scores across years as if difficulty were identical.
