# Institutes and programmes accepting GATE DA

This is a programme shortlist based on the most recent published rules available on 6 October 2026. Admission rules for the 2027 intake can change. Check the new brochure for qualifying degree, minimum marks, score cutoff, mode (TA/RA), written test/interview, and application deadline. A GATE DA score is not automatically eligible for every MTech CSE programme.

| Institute | Programme | Evidence from published rules |
|---|---|---|
| IIT Madras | MTech Data Science and AI | 2026 MTech brochure maps its DA1 programme to GATE DA; CS graduate degree is listed as an eligible qualifying discipline. https://mtechadm.iitm.ac.in/pdfs/Mtech_IB_2026.pdf |
| IISc Bengaluru | MTech Artificial Intelligence | 2026–27 FAQ accepts GATE CS, DA, EC, EE; in-person written test also used. https://kiac.iisc.ac.in/academics/mtech-ai/frequently-asked-questions/ |
| IIT Hyderabad | MTech Artificial Intelligence | TA1/RA1 GATE modes list CS, EC, EE, DA, MA, subject to degree rules. https://ai.iith.ac.in/m-tech-admissions/index.html |
| IIT Guwahati | MTech Data Science | Lists DA and CS among accepted GATE papers. https://www.iitg.ac.in/ds/mtech_ds_program.html |
| IIT Roorkee | MTech Data Science | 2026 brochure includes DA and CS in the programme's main GATE disciplines. It also lists DA among other disciplines for MTech AI. https://www.iitr.ac.in/Academics/static/Admission/PG/M.Tech/2026/Regular/Information_Brochure_2026.pdf |
| IIT Kharagpur | MTech Artificial Intelligence | 2026–27 brochure says admission can be based on DA, CS, EC, EE. https://gateoffice.iitkgp.ac.in/jmp/d/docs/JMP_Brochure_2026_27.pdf |

Practical strategy: keep CS as the route to traditional CSE options and DA as an additional route to AI/DS programmes. Shortlist a specific programme, not merely an institute name. Use your GATE scores and the fresh 2027 admission brochures in March–April 2027 to finalise applications.

Official GATE notice on institute-specific admission criteria: https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/notifications
