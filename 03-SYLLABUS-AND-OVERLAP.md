Official 2027 checklists: CS https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf ; DA https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/DA_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf ; GA https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/GA_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf .

This is a comparison of published lists, not an official changelog. Check the 2027 PDFs for exam scope.

### CS 2027 wording to notice

- Digital Logic explicitly names algebraic, Karnaugh-map, and tabular minimisation.
- COA explicitly names ALU design, hardwired/microprogrammed control, memory interfacing, cache mapping/performance.
- Networks uses a tighter list. ARP, DHCP, ICMP, UDP, SMTP and FTP are named in 2026 but not explicitly in 2027. Do not assume all related concepts vanish; prioritise the 2027 list.
- Other CS sections remain: Engineering Mathematics, C/data structures, Algorithms, TOC, Compiler, OS, Databases.

### DA 2027 sections

1. Probability and Statistics: events, Bayes, expectations, distributions, CLT, intervals, z/t/chi-squared tests.
2. Linear Algebra: vector spaces, matrices, rank/nullity, projections, eigenvalues, LU and SVD.
3. Calculus and Optimization: single-variable functions, Taylor series, extrema.
4. Python, Data Structures and Algorithms: standard structures, sorting/searching, graph basics.
5. Database Management and Warehousing: relational topics, indexing, transformations, multidimensional models.
6. Machine Learning: regression/classification, common models, validation, clustering, PCA, neural basics.
7. AI: informed/uninformed/adversarial search, logic, uncertainty and inference.

## Overlap map

| Shared concepts — study once | DA-specific depth | CS-specific depth |
|---|---|---|
| GA; probability; basic statistics; matrices/eigenvalues; single-variable calculus; stacks/queues/lists/trees; searching/sorting/graph traversal/shortest path; ER/relational model/SQL/normal forms/indexing | Statistical tests and additional distributions; SVD; Python; warehouse; ML; AI search/inference | Discrete mathematics; C; deeper algorithms; Digital Logic; COA; OS; TOC; Compiler; Networks |

No official overlap percentage or shared-mark guarantee exists. GA is 15 marks in each paper. Practise common concepts using questions from both papers because style and depth vary.
